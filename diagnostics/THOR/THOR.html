THOR: ocean dynamical regime classification

Classifies each ocean grid point into one of 6 dynamical regimes, using a pretrained 50-model MLP ensemble driven by 8 physical features derived from sea surface height, wind stress, and bathymetry (Yik et al. 2023, THOR-MOM6). Alongside the regime map, THOR reports a per-point prediction entropy: how much the 50 ensemble members agree, as a confidence companion to the majority-vote classification.
